Promotion Criteria

  
An item moves from Research to Master Archive when:

  
  

    

      
📄

      
Official Documentation

      
Catalog listing, press release, or official product page confirms existence

    

    

      
🏷️

      
Verified Tags

      
Clear photos of tush tag showing manufacturer and product code

    

    

      
👥

      
Multiple Examples

      
2+ independent examples with matching characteristics

    

    

      
✓

      
No Conflicts

      
No evidence suggesting bootleg, custom, or duplicate entry
